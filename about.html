06 // INQUIRIES & PROTOCOL

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01

How are creator profiles reviewed?

Celebso X focuses on authentic work and professional identity. Profiles may be reviewed based on the quality of work, experience, skills, achievements, and the information shared by the creator. Our goal is to maintain a professional and trusted community.

02

What criteria apply to Brand Partnerships?

Celebso X works with brands, companies, organizations, creators, and professionals looking to build meaningful collaborations. Partnerships can include campaigns, projects, content creation, talent discovery, and long-term professional opportunities.

03

Are in-person meetings supported?

Yes. In-person meetings may be available at Celebso locations by confirmed appointment. Submit a request through the contact form with your preferred location, purpose, and availability.

04

Where should press and media requests be directed?

For press, media, interviews, partnership inquiries, or high-resolution brand assets, select “Press / Media” in the contact form or reach out directly at info@celebso.com. For collaborations, media, and opportunities — connect with us directly.
