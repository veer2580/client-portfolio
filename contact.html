02 / PROTOCOL FAQ

COMMUNICATION CLARIFICATIONS

DOCS // FREQUENT INQUIRIES • REV 2026.04

01

How are creator profile applications vetted?

Our curatorial committee reviews verified work portfolios, published bodies of work, and professional accreditation. We do not evaluate social vanity metrics; merit, craft discipline, and creative contribution take priority.

02

Can international brands hire creators through Celebso X?

Yes. Celebso X supports global agency and brand commissions. Invoicing accommodates multi-currency settlements (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, and INR) with statutory compliance and automated tax escrow.

03

What are the platform commission structures and fees?

Celebso X charges zero listing fees for vetted creators. Commissioned client briefs maintain a transparent 5% platform protocol fee covering verified contract escrow, dispute arbitration, and lossless media hosting.

04

How does milestone escrow and contract settlement work?

Funds for client briefs are held in automated milestone-based escrow accounts before production commences. Release triggers upon verified milestone sign-off, protecting creative time and capital.

05

Is my portfolio data fully exportable and sovereign?

Absolutely. Celebso X champions data sovereignty. Creators retain 100% intellectual copyright over all hosted works, and can export high-resolution media, case studies, and provenance certificates in standard open formats anytime.

06

How do creators apply for the Celebso Fellows Residency?

Applications for the biannual residency cohort open every April and October. Selection is conducted by the Curatorial Council, awarding studio grants, exhibition pavilions in Mumbai and Bengaluru, and hardware stipends.
